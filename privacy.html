Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Overview

Chrome Auto Screenshot is a personal-use Chrome extension designed to capture screenshots of the active Chrome window and upload them to the user's authorized Google Drive account.

Information Collected

The extension captures screenshots of the active Chrome tab, which may contain personal or sensitive information.

How Information Is Used

Screenshots are compressed into WebP format and uploaded directly to the user's Google Drive account. The extension does not intentionally upload screenshots to any other service.

Data Storage

Screenshots are stored in the user's Google Drive account. Extension settings and authentication credentials may be stored locally in the browser or extension files.

Data Sharing

The extension does not sell screenshot data or share it with advertisers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Screenshots remain in Google Drive until deleted by the account owner. Screenshot capture can be stopped through the extension dashboard.

Google API Services

The extension uses Google Drive API access to upload screenshots to the authorized Google Drive account.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
