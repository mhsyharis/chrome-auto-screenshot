Chrome Auto Screenshot

A personal-use Chrome extension that captures screenshots of the active Chrome window and uploads them to the user's own Google Drive.

Features

  • Automatic screenshots every 30 seconds while Chrome is active.
  • WebP image compression.
  • Direct uploads to Google Drive.
  • Start, pause and stop controls.

Privacy

This extension is intended for personal use only. Screenshots are uploaded to the authorized user's Google Drive account.
